# 图 2 营收对比 — Q 版数据海报

## 推荐风格
- 风格：Q 版数据海报（延续封面 Q 版吉祥物 + 黑底霓虹黄）
- 为什么选它：账号历史"纯数据图表 = 冷启失败"，必须叠 Q 版角色当锚点；同时延续封面风格
- 这张图最该卖什么：**€130M → €200M+ 的视觉跳跃 + "一年涨 50%" 一句话判断**

## Story Atoms
- 主角：1 个 Q 版健身吉祥物（中央，扛着钱标签往柱子上爬）
- 动作瞬间：双手抱着 €200M 大金块往柱顶冲
- 情绪冲突：从 €130M 黑色短柱跳到 €200M 霓虹黄高柱（视觉差距 = 涨幅）
- 背景符号：净色面板背景 + 极简 Hyrox 角标

## Final Page Prompt
```text
Magazine-style infographic page for fitness racing brand HYROX, 3:4 vertical layout, Q-style mascot data poster.

LAYOUT:
- Top 20%: Bold large Chinese headline "一年涨 50%" in neon yellow on black background, with subtitle small white text "Hyrox 营收 一年翻倍" beneath
- Middle 60%: Two vertical bar columns side by side
  - LEFT shorter bar (40% canvas height): solid black with thick neon yellow outline, label below "2025 €130M" in white, with smaller text "约 ¥10 亿" beneath
  - RIGHT taller bar (75% canvas height): solid neon yellow, label below "2026 预计 €200M+" in black on yellow strip, with smaller text "约 ¥15.5 亿" beneath
  - One Q-style chibi mascot character climbing the right yellow bar mid-way, dough-round body, short stubby limbs, thick black outline, simple dot eyes, hugging a large gold coin labeled "€200M" with both arms, smiling determined, wearing black tank top with neon yellow Hyrox accent
- Bottom 20%: small footer in muted gray text "数据来源：Infront 集团披露 + 第三方推算  |  Hyrox 未上市，2026 数字为预计值" — small enough to not steal focus

COLOR PALETTE: pure black background + neon yellow brand color + white text + small gold accent.

STYLE: clean infographic poster, Q-style mascot adds personality, no extra clutter, sticker-friendly. Avoid pie charts, multiple data series, axis labels, or anything that looks like a slide deck.

--ar 3:4 --stylize 250
```

## 如果要继续改
- 数字层级要保住：「一年涨 50%」(头条) > 「€200M+」(主柱顶) > 「¥15.5 亿」(辅助换算)
- 不要动什么：Q 版小人 dough-body + 黑底霓虹黄配色（视觉锚）
- 风险：如果 Q 版小人显得太 cute 削弱"商业感"，可以把小人改成戴墨镜 / 西装外套版本
