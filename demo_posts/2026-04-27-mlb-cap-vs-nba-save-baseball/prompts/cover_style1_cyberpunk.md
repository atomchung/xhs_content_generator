# MLB 帽封面 prompt — Style 1：Cyberpunk 霓虹夜街

## 推荐风格

- 风格：Cyberpunk Shanghai night street（霓虹街拍 + 韩中双语招牌 + 未来噪点 / 雾霾蓝紫色）
- 为什么选它：把「衣服已经从体育里独立出来，变成都市潮流符号」这件事用最强烈的视觉语言讲出来 — 霓虹 logo 比球场更有存在感本身就是 punchline
- 这张图最该卖什么：**这顶 MLB 帽已经不属于棒球场了，它属于霓虹街道**

## Final Prompt

```text
A 3:4 vertical magazine-style cover. Foreground close-up: a young Chinese person in their early 20s wearing a black MLB cap with the iconic crisp white "MLB" wordmark glowing under street neon, head slightly tilted down, eyes catching reflected magenta and cyan light, oversized streetwear hoodie, shoulders cropped at lower frame edge. Hyper-detailed comic illustration style with crisp ink linework — physically-rendered manga figure with photo-accurate East-Asian likeness, rendered as comic illustration (not photo).

Background: cyberpunk Shanghai-Seoul hybrid alleyway at night, dense holographic Korean and Chinese signage overhead (no English text in signage), wet asphalt reflecting magenta and cyan neon, distant blurred crowd silhouettes, light haze and atmospheric depth, layered light pollution, slight CRT scanline texture overlay.

Color palette: deep navy + electric magenta + cyan accent + a single warm amber rim-light on the cap brim. The MLB wordmark on the cap is the brightest single light source in frame.

Composition: face at upper-left third, MLB cap is the visual anchor at center-top, neon vanishing point trailing into background lower-right, leaving clean negative space at top-center for headline.

TEXT OVERLAY (clean bold sans-serif, must read clearly against neon):
- Top headline (massive Chinese, neon-white with thin magenta outer glow): 「中国一千家 MLB 店」 — top 18% of canvas
- Subline directly below (medium Chinese, soft white): 「背后没一个美国人」
- Bottom-right tiny corner mark (faint cyan): small "F&F" mono wordmark, low opacity

STYLE: cyberpunk neon manga cover illustration, magazine-grade composition, Akira / Blade Runner 2049 lighting cues, clean ink linework, comic illustration not photo.

--ar 3:4 --stylize 250
```

## 如果要继续改

- 背景优先改什么：招牌可以从「韩中混合」收紧到「全韩文」（更扣 F&F 韩国背景）；或反向放大「全中文」（更扣中国市场属性） — 二选一不要混
- 不要动什么：MLB 字样作为画面最亮单一光源；霓虹色限定在 magenta + cyan + 一束 amber rim，多色彩会糊掉
- 配色权重要保住：MLB 帽 logo > 标题字 > 招牌噪点，三层视觉权重不能反过来
