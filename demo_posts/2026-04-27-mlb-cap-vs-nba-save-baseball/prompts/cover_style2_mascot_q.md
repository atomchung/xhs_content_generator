# MLB 帽封面 prompt — Style 2：小红薯 Q 版吉祥物

## 推荐风格

- 风格：实况野球豆薯感 Q 版 mascot，圆豆身、短手短脚、厚黑描边、点状黑眼，棒球场拼贴
- 为什么选它：账号既有 Q 版风格资产（hyrox 那一篇 2945 views），轻知识 / 反差科普题用 Q 版能把数字讲软；用一只戴 MLB 帽的 Q 版小棒球手撞上「冷场的棒球场」，反差不需要解释就能读懂
- 这张图最该卖什么：**「衣服热闹 vs 比赛冷清」的并置反差，用最不严肃的画风讲最反讽的事**

## Final Prompt

```text
A 3:4 vertical Q-style mascot illustration cover poster, official sport-fashion brand poster aesthetic, sticker-friendly clean composition.

Subject: a single chibi Q-style baseball player mascot at center, dough-round body, short stubby limbs, thick black outline, simple dot eyes, minimal facial features, friendly smiling expression. The mascot wears an oversized black baseball cap with a crisp white "MLB" wordmark on the front (the cap is comically large relative to the head, drawing eye to the logo). The mascot also wears a streetwear hoodie in soft cream color with one yellow Hyrox-style accent stripe — explicitly streetwear styling, not a baseball uniform. One hand holds a small baseball bat resting casually on shoulder, the other hand in a hoodie pocket. Tilted three-quarter pose, friendly and confident.

Background: split-frame composition.
- Left half (warm side): mini cute illustrated street scene — three more tiny mascots in different hoodies all wearing MLB caps, walking past a cartoon storefront with a soft "MLB" sign, warm cream + soft red palette, busy and lively.
- Right half (cool side): a tiny empty cartoon baseball stadium with empty bleachers, a single lonely Q-mascot pitcher on the mound under cool blue light, no spectators, soft desaturated blue palette, quiet and empty.

A vertical thin dotted divider line separates the two halves to make the contrast unmissable.

TEXT OVERLAY (rounded bold Chinese sans-serif, sticker-style with thick white outline):
- Top headline (large neon-red Chinese): 「一千家 MLB 店」 — top 16% of canvas
- Subheadline directly below (medium black Chinese): 「背后没一个美国人」
- Tiny corner sticker bottom-right: small round badge with "F&F" mono wordmark in pastel mint

COLOR PALETTE: cream + soft warm red + pastel mint + cool desaturated blue (only on the right half) + black outline.

STYLE: clean Q-style mascot illustration, kid-book friendly, sport-fashion brand event poster polish, no clutter, no extra props beyond the storefront and stadium, thick consistent black outlines, dot eyes everywhere.

--ar 3:4 --stylize 250
```

## 如果要继续改

- 背景优先改什么：左半幅热闹街景的 mascot 数量（3 → 5 加密度）；右半幅可以从「空看台」换成「冷清的转播屏 + 一个没人看的客厅」
- 不要动什么：中央主 mascot 的 dough-body + 超大 MLB 帽 + 街头 hoodie（不是棒球队服 — 这是核心 punchline）；左右双色温分轨
- 视觉权重：MLB 帽 logo > 中央 mascot > 标题字 > 左右场景
