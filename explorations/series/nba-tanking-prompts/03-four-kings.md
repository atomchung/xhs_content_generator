# 图 3 — 四大天王数据卡

## 推荐风格
- 风格：Editorial Collage（体育商业杂志拼贴）
- 为什么选它：四个人物 + 各自数据需要高密度排版，Editorial Collage 能把人物和数字同时服务一个结论："这四个人真的值得整个联盟疯狂"
- 这张图最该卖什么：信息结构——四个人各自的最强标签一眼看完

## Final Prompt

```text
Vertical 3:4 editorial sports magazine collage layout, dark background.

Four-quadrant grid composition, each quadrant featuring one basketball player:

Top-left quadrant: Young athletic forward, age ~19, 6'9", in a dynamic dunk pose captured mid-air. Stylized anime-influenced rendering. Bold large floating number "25.5" in white with golden glow beside him. Small label text in clean sans-serif. Dark jersey with subtle purple accents. Background: abstract paint splash in purple/gold.

Top-right quadrant: Young guard, age ~19, in explosive crossover drive pose, low and fast. Bold floating number "19.9" in white. A small trophy/award icon suggesting MVP-caliber. White jersey with crimson accents. Background: abstract paint splash in crimson/blue.

Bottom-left quadrant: Young forward, age ~19, in a smooth shooting form, ball releasing from fingertips. Bold floating number "20.2" in white. A small "Player of the Year" badge icon. Blue jersey. Background: abstract paint splash in royal blue/white.

Bottom-right quadrant: Young big forward, age ~19, 6'10", in a powerful shot-blocking pose, arm extended high. Bold floating numbers "19.8 / 9.4" in white. Light blue jersey. Background: abstract paint splash in light blue/white.

Dividing lines between quadrants: thin golden lines forming a cross pattern.

Center intersection: a subtle golden crown or diamond icon where the four quadrants meet, suggesting "four kings."

Overall style: premium sports magazine double-page spread aesthetic. Each quadrant has consistent composition — player action on one side, oversized stat number on the other. Anime-influenced player rendering with sharp line work but editorial layout sensibility. Typography is bold, modern sans-serif. Color palette: dark navy base, each quadrant has its own accent color splash. Subtle halftone dot texture in backgrounds for print magazine feel.

No real player likenesses. Generic athletic young players with distinct builds matching their positions.
```
