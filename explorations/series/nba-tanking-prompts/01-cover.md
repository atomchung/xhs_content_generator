## Final Prompt

```text
Vertical 3:4 shonen sports anime key visual. Four young basketball players standing in a hero lineup facing the viewer, arranged shoulder-to-shoulder but staggered in depth. They radiate completely different physical presences and energy signatures. Dark NBA arena behind them, hazy spotlights cutting through smoke, a distant scoreboard glowing a dim "17-64" in red.

FIGURE 1 (second from left, tallest): A 6'9" lean, spider-limbed forward, age 19. Impossibly long arms and legs, narrow waist, wiry muscle definition — built like a young Kevin Durant. He holds a basketball in his right hand above his head effortlessly, arm fully extended, as if about to tomahawk dunk on thin air. His body is the tallest vertical line in the composition. Expression: locked-in, predatory focus. Jersey: dark purple with gold trim. A golden-amber flame aura rises from his shoulders upward, licking the top edge of the frame.

FIGURE 2 (far left, shortest): A compact, explosively built guard, age 19. Noticeably shorter than the others — maybe 6'2" — but the most densely muscled, with a low center of gravity like a coiled spring. He's in a half-crouch, weight on his front foot, ball low in his left hand as if frozen mid-crossover. His silhouette is horizontal and compressed where Figure 1's is vertical and stretched. Expression: fierce, jaw clenched, eyes burning. Jersey: deep crimson with blue accents. Electric blue lightning crackles around his hands and feet, fragmenting the floor beneath him.

FIGURE 3 (second from right, medium height): A balanced, polished forward, age 19. Proportioned like an idealized athlete — broad shoulders, lean but not skinny, the most "finished" build of the four. He stands the most upright and composed, basketball tucked under one arm casually. His posture reads calm confidence while the others radiate raw intensity. Expression: slight knowing smirk, the only one who looks relaxed. Jersey: royal blue with white trim. A cold white-frost aura surrounds him subtly, condensation-like particles floating near his skin, controlled and precise.

FIGURE 4 (far right, second tallest and WIDEST): A 6'10" power forward with an exaggerated wingspan, age 19. His defining visual trait is width — shoulders like a coat hanger, arms that stretch unnaturally far from his body. He stands with both arms slightly spread at his sides, palms open, fingers extended — the "wingspan display" pose. His silhouette is the widest in the group by far. Expression: quiet intensity, stoic. Jersey: Carolina blue with white. A green shockwave energy ripples outward from his body like a force field, emphasizing his massive physical reach.

COMPOSITION: The four figures are NOT equal size. Figure 1 (tallest/lean) and Figure 4 (widest) are slightly forward and larger. Figures 2 and 3 are slightly behind. This creates a diamond depth arrangement, not a flat line. Each figure's energy aura bleeds slightly into the next, but their silhouettes never overlap — even as shadows, you could tell all four apart instantly.

BACKGROUND: Dark arena interior, dramatic god-rays of white spotlight cutting through blue-gray haze. Upper deck seats are visible but mostly empty. A single distant scoreboard shows "17-64" in dim red — the losing teams' shame hanging over the arena. The floor is reflective, catching faint colored reflections from each player's aura.

STYLE: Shonen sports anime à la Slam Dunk or Blue Lock key visual. Bold ink-like outlines, cel-shaded with dramatic rim lighting. Each player's color palette is strictly separated — no color bleed between characters. Speed lines and particle effects are specific to each figure's energy type (fire/lightning/frost/shockwave). High contrast. Clean negative space between figures. Premium anime production quality — this is the season's final episode key art.

ABSOLUTE REQUIREMENTS:
- Four clearly different body types (lean-tall / compact-short / balanced-medium / wide-tall)
- Four clearly different color palettes (purple-gold / crimson-blue / royal blue-white / Carolina blue-green)
- Four clearly different energy effects (rising flames / crackling lightning / cold frost / expanding shockwave)
- No text overlays
- 3:4 vertical aspect ratio
```
