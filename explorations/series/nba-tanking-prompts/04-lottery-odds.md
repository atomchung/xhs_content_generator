# 图 4 — 乐透概率收尾页

## 推荐风格
- 风格：Minimal Data Poster（极简数据海报）
- 为什么选它：收尾页只需传达一个冲击——14% 的概率。数字本身就是最好的互动钩子，极简海报让这个反差最大化
- 这张图最该卖什么：情绪——输了 64 场换来的只是 14%

## Final Prompt

```text
Vertical 3:4 minimal data poster, dramatic dark composition.

Center of frame: a massive "14%" in bold distressed white typography, taking up approximately 40% of the image width. The percentage sign slightly smaller than the numbers. Subtle golden glow emanating from behind the text, creating a spotlight effect.

Above the 14%: small clean white text in modern sans-serif, equivalent to "No. 1 pick probability" — understated, secondary hierarchy.

Below the 14%: a thin horizontal golden line, then below it in smaller muted gray text, the equivalent of "Lost 64 games for this" — creating an emotional gut-punch contrast.

Background: deep dark navy blue, almost black. At the very bottom of the frame, barely visible in very low opacity: silhouettes of basketball players sitting on a bench with heads down, suggesting defeated players on a losing team. This should be extremely subtle, almost like a watermark.

Top of frame: very small, subtle text showing the date "5.10" (lottery date) in golden text, like a countdown marker.

Overall aesthetic: the entire poster is built around the emotional weight of that single number. Brutalist typography meets sports editorial. Maximum negative space. The contrast between the huge effort (64 losses) and the small probability (14%) is the entire message.

Color palette: near-black navy background, white primary text, muted gold accents, very subtle gray for secondary text. No bright colors. Somber, contemplative mood — is it worth it?

No busy elements. No decorative graphics. Pure typographic impact.
```

## 如果要继续改
- 背景优先改什么：底部剪影的透明度、是否加入乐透抽签机的元素
- 不要动什么：14% 作为绝对视觉中心的设定
