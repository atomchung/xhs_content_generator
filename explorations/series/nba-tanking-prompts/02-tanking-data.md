# 图 2 — 擺爛数据页

## 推荐风格
- 风格：Minimal Data Poster（极简数据海报）
- 为什么选它：这页的主角是数字（17-64、39 连败、50 万罚款），不是人物。极简海报让数字冲击力最大化
- 这张图最该卖什么：信息结构——擺爛的荒谬程度用三组数字一目了然

## Final Prompt

```text
Vertical 3:4 minimal data poster, dark navy blue background with subtle basketball court line pattern barely visible.

Layout — three horizontal data blocks stacked vertically, each block separated by thin glowing red divider lines:

Top block: Large distressed bold white text "17-64" as the dominant visual element, taking up ~30% width. Below it in smaller golden text: team city name. A subtle downward red arrow icon beside the record.

Middle block: Massive glowing red number "39" centered, with smaller white text beneath reading the equivalent of "games lost in a row combined" in clean sans-serif. Three small team shield silhouettes in a row below.

Bottom block: Dollar sign icon in gold, followed by large white bold text "$500,000" — with a small red stamp-like overlay suggesting "FINED" in angular distressed type. Below in small text: the reason for the fine.

Overall aesthetic: clean, dark, editorial data visualization. No photographs, no player imagery. Numbers are the absolute heroes. Typography-driven layout with generous negative space. Color palette: navy blue background, white primary text, gold accents, red for negative/warning elements. Subtle grain texture overlay for print-quality feel.

No decorative elements beyond the data. No busy backgrounds. Think: ESPN or The Athletic premium infographic meets brutalist poster design.
```

## 如果要继续改
- 背景优先改什么：底色深浅、分隔线样式
- 不要动什么：三组数据的层级顺序（战绩 → 连败 → 罚款）
