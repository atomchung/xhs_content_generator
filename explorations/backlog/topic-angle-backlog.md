## Review Rules

- `active now`: worth revisiting in the next ideation round
- `watch later`: good idea, but not urgent yet
- `parked`: keep the note, but stop spending attention on it for now
- every ideation round can raise, lower, or park entries instead of recreating them from scratch

## Promotion Rule

When one topic is chosen for real work:

1. Scaffold `demo_posts/<date>-<slug>/`
2. Build `research/fact_pack.md`
3. Turn the research into `research/story_spine.md`
4. Draft the final post in `text/post.md`
